This is a shell for Hyprland inspired by Omarchy using Quickshell. It has a notification manager, clipboard manager, wallpaper switcher, theme switcher, an app launcher, and popups for WiFi and sound. This was made entirely using GPT 6.1 Sol and GPT 6 Astra. I understand the general structure of the program and make a few straight-forwards edits, but I am not a programmer and not at all well-versed in QML.
I made this after a lot of back and forth with myself about whether I should be using an LLM for this (at one point, I even decided to learn Quickshell for this, but I quickly ran out of motivation) and if I should know the entire code-base, I bit the bullet when I got a free subscription to ChatGPT Plus for a month and just decided to go for it. Initially, I decided to go step by step, and add one component at a time, but it was taking a while. After a few days of back and forth, I finally abandoned that approach and decided to one-shot it. This approach surprisingly worked much better, and the final output was much more functional; however, it came at the expense of my understanding of the code.
As of this moment, I am as content as one can be with the output, the functionality is exactly what I wanted it to be. Everybody is free to use this and even audit it, if they want to help out a stranger.
